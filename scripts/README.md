# TEP-WB Analysis Scripts

This folder contains the reproducible analysis pipeline for Paper 13.

## Structure

```
scripts/
├── steps/           # Numbered analysis steps
│   ├── step_000_*.py # Data acquisition (catalog download)
│   ├── step_001_*.py # Sample selection
│   ├── step_002_*.py # Kinematic analysis
│   └── ...
└── utils/           # Shared utilities
```

## Execution

Steps should be run in numerical order. Each step produces outputs in `results/outputs/`.

## Recent steps (closure audit, 2025-09)

- `step_017_radius_law_estimator_check.py` — canonical radius-law estimator through the embedded propagator; ambient-vertex conventions and environmental supply/demand.
- `step_019_two_branch_estimator.py` — two-branch sector through the same estimator (kept for the record; superseded by step_020's corrected ambient semantics).
- `step_020_scalar_shear_ambient.py` — corrected estimator: the embedded boundary condition u0 is the scalar-shear gradient derived self-consistently from g_obs = g_t·u_bar·(P_X+2) at the solar circle (u0 = 0.163 two-branch / 0.207 baseline / 0.213 exp_interp — not the total-acceleration 0.57); pure-propagator reading (the resolved solve carries ambient suppression once); isotropic deprojection convolution. Two-branch: alpha = 0.371 vs observed 0.366 (0.44sigma), R_s = 3,676 vs 2,646 AU (1.69sigma), joint chi2 = 3.06; baseline and exp_interp excluded at ~30sigma on amplitude. Side prediction: implied baryonic share of v_c^2 at R_sun = 48% (two-branch) vs 34% (baseline). Outputs `results/outputs/020_scalar_shear_ambient.json`.
- `step_020b_run_pair_at_derived.py` — two-centre pair solve at the derived ambient u0 = 0.163 (parallel-channel, pessimistic mid-transition: stars nested inside each other's compressed axial wake). Outputs `results/outputs/020b_pair_profile_derived_u0.json`.
- `step_020c_refined_estimator.py` — refined joint test: per-bin mass marginalisation from the real catalogue (corr(log M, log s) = 0.38), ambient heterogeneity, the orientation-averaged pair response <y> = (2/3)y_perp + (1/3)y_par, the per-star scale r*_i = sqrt(G m_i/g_t) (transition sqrt(2) earlier than the total-mass convention), and baseline-window sensitivity of the observed fit (innermost-bin unresolved-companion dilution). Headline: alpha = 0.347 vs observed 0.346–0.366, R_s = 2,940 AU vs observed 2,646–3,168 AU depending on normalisation window — joint pass at ~1.5sigma / ~0.5sigma. Outputs `results/outputs/020c_refined_estimator.json`.
- `step_020d_disk_ambient.py` — environmental-ordering channel budget: planar disk solve under the two-branch flux law (global shear grows with z — wrong sign), local kpc-scale generated field (~1.1x contrast), density projections (rho^{1/3}->1.35, rho^{1/2}->1.56, rho^1->2.44) vs required ~1.8; per-stratum joint inversion: each stratum closes with a single ambient (midplane u0=0.20 -> a=0.236/R=2,612 vs 0.244/2,821; halo u0=0.11 -> a=0.414/R=4,514 vs 0.401/4,681). Outputs `results/outputs/020d_disk_ambient.json`.
- `step_020e_pair_at_strata.py` — two-centre pair profiles at the stratum ambients u0 = 0.10 (halo) and 0.23 (midplane); pair suppression deepens with ambient, steepening the alpha(u0) map. Outputs `results/outputs/020e_pair_profile_strata.json`.
- `step_020f_pair_grid.py` — pair profiles at intermediate ambients u0 = 0.14/0.17/0.20, filling the interpolation grid for the ambient map. Outputs `results/outputs/020f_pair_profile_grid.json`.
- `step_020g_ambient_map.py` — empirical ambient profile u_eff(|Z|): builds the (alpha, R_s) forward maps over the ambient grid, refits the five |Z| quintile strata with free (alpha, R_s), and inverts each parameter independently. alpha channel gives a monotone declining u_eff(z) = 0.21 -> 0.10 over |Z| = 22 -> 347 pc, empirical law u_eff ~ rho^{0.40} after per-stratum mass-kernel correction (strata differ in mass 1.11 -> 1.38 Msun); alpha and Rs inversions agree at the endpoints (~0.21/~0.20 midplane, ~0.12/~0.08 halo) but diverge mid-strata. Outputs `results/outputs/020g_ambient_map.json`.
- `step_020h_depth_channel.py` — field-depth ambient channel, tested and rejected: integrated u_depth(z) matches rho^0.52 only as an outer-boundary artifact (exponent sweeps 1.30 -> 0.11 over z_max = 400 -> 2000 pc), local-window depth has the wrong sign, and the depth amplitude (~5e-9 well units) cannot act as a starvation ambient. Measured law u_eff ~ rho^0.51 stands; mechanism class remains the density-coupled in-medium truncation (AUD-3 constraint slice). Outputs `results/outputs/020h_depth_channel.json`.
- `step_020i_noise_null.py` — noise-aware Newtonian null: per-pair Gaia proper-motion errors carried through the Rice-rectifying |Δμ| magnitude into the step_012 orbital sampler (four eccentricity laws), per-stratum noise-rectified null profiles, an unbiased absolute-noise-floor subsample test (σ_ṽ cuts that do not condition on observed ṽ), and a same-noise-kernel joint likelihood comparing boosted (α, R_s) vs pure-Newtonian predictions. The noise-aware null is rejected at Δχ² = 34–2249 in 19 of 20 stratum×e-law cells; the detection and endpoint environmental ordering survive. Outputs `results/outputs/020i_noise_null.json`.
- `step_020j_noise_corrected_ambient.py` — ambient inversion through the noise kernel: each candidate u0 is forward-modelled through per-pair noise and χ²-compared to observed per-stratum profiles. Endpoint ordering survives (u_eff ≈ 0.60 midplane vs ≈ 0.18 halo); mid-strata non-monotone with large χ². Outputs `results/outputs/020j_noise_corrected_ambient.json`.
- `step_020k_selection_downturn.py` — selection-cut origin of the widest-bin downturn: rebuilds the kinematic pool from the raw catalog with all step_001 cuts except the R_chance_align threshold, then compares per-stratum profiles at R_chance < 0.01/0.05/0.20/none. Profiles are identical for s ≲ 9 kAU and diverge above — the baseline cut removes ~20% of pairs at 8–30 kAU preferentially from the high-ṽ tail, manufacturing the turnover. Resolves the mid-strata fit-family tension. Outputs `results/outputs/020k_selection_downturn.json`.
- `step_020l_selection_window_inversion.py` — definitive ambient inversion on the selection-insensitive window (s < 9 kAU): noise-aware TEP forward model over a dense u0 grid, chi2 under both per-stratum self-normalisation (diagnostic — shown to manufacture a spurious mid-strata Newtonian preference via noise-inflated anchor bins) and absolute medians (headline). Absolute-profile inversion is clean and monotone under every realistic eccentricity law: thermal u_eff = 0.18 -> <0.08 over |Z| = 22 -> 346 pc with Newtonian rejected at dchi2 = 1589–2787 per stratum; corrected exponent ~0.5 vs ln rho_disk. Outputs `results/outputs/020l_selection_window.json`.
- `step_020l_selection_safe_environment.py` — model-free environmental coupling on the selection-safe window: per-pair |Z| regression with sep×distance fixed effects + within-cell permutation null (+6.7σ at R<0.01, +17σ at R<0.002, monotone-stronger with purity), median ordering matrix, and the covariate-hardened separation-resolved test (cells fixed on separation, distance decile, noise-floor decile, mass decile): the coupling is separation-localised — flat at s<1.5 kAU, rising to +13.0σ at s=5–9 kAU (+12.2σ with feh). z5−z1 bootstrap contrast +0.043 ± 0.006 (6.9σ). Outputs `results/outputs/020l_selection_safe_environment.json`.
- `step_020m_eccentricity_mixture.py` — free eccentricity-mixture (thermal/superthermal/uniform weights) + ambient u0 joint fit on the s<9kAU window with common random numbers and the measured PM-error noise kernel. Every stratum selects the uniform-dominated component; absolute profiles fit at chi2/dof ~1-8; ambient ordering monotone (u0: 0.40->0.15); density exponent ~0.7 under the data-preferred mixture (e-law systematic spans 0.5-0.7); noise-aware Newtonian null rejected at chi2 ~800-2000/stratum. Outputs `results/outputs/020m_eccentricity_mixture.json`.
- `step_020n_purity_weighted_profiles.py` — selection-consistent profile estimator: replaces the hard R_chance_align < 0.01 cut with per-pair bound-probability weights w = 1 - R_chance_align on the full pool. The weighted profiles rise monotonically to ~1.6-1.8 at 30 kAU in every stratum (downturn entirely removed; hard-cut comparison declines by 0.04-0.08). Absolute weighted medians show the environmental ordering growing with separation: z5-z1 contrast +0.018 at 2-5 kAU rising to +0.093 at 15-30 kAU. Canonical saturating-exponential fits on the weighted profiles hit the R_s upper bound in the dilute strata — the response is unsaturated at the widest measured separations, so quoted transition radii from hard-cut samples are lower-bounded effective values. Outputs `results/outputs/020n_purity_weighted_profiles.json`.
- `step_020o_jacobi_tide_bound.py` — per-pair Jacobi-radius tidal bound under the solar-circle tidal tensor (r_J = (M_bin/3 M_enc)^{1/3} R_GC, M_enc = 1e11 M_sun, R_GC = 8.2 kpc; Mamajek masses as in the pipeline pool). r_J 10/50/90 percentiles = 221/279/323 kAU; the response window s = 5-9 kAU sits at median s/r_J = 0.023 with 0.0% of pairs beyond s/r_J = 0.1; the 9-30 kAU bin contributes 3.5% beyond 0.1; beyond ~30 kAU the tide dominates (median s/r_J = 0.13). Outputs `results/outputs/020o_jacobi_tide_bound.json`.
